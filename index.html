Why QT Matcha?

Wellness should fit into your life. QT is a fruit-forward functional matcha drink that makes a daily ritual as simple as putting a packet in your water bottle.

Ingredients & allergens

QT combines real matcha with iron, vitamin C, and collagen. Full ingredient and allergen details will be shared before launch.

What does QT taste like?

QT tastes like delicious matcha with bright fruit flavor. Choose Strawberry, White Peach, or Lemon Yuzu for a refreshing twist on the matcha you love.

How do I make it?

Pour one packet into 8–12 oz of water. Shake or mix well, then sip and enjoy.
