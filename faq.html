THE PRODUCT

What is QT?

What flavors does QT come in?

Three at launch: Strawberry, White Peach, and Lemon Yuzu. Same matcha base in every packet, infused with iron, vitamin C, and collagen. The Taster has one packet of each; the Variety Pack has ten of each.

Does it taste like a supplement?

No. QT is a fruit-forward matcha drink first — real matcha with bright fruit flavor. The iron, vitamin C, and collagen are along for the ride, not the headline on your tongue.

How do I make it?

Tear one packet into 8–12 oz of water and shake or stir. It dissolves without a whisk. Cold water and ice are the fan favorite; it works with your milk of choice too.

How much caffeine is in a packet?

QT is made with real matcha, so each packet contains caffeine. The exact amount per serving will be printed on the nutrition panel and published here before ordering opens.

Is QT gluten free? Is it vegan?

QT is gluten free. It is not vegan — collagen is an animal-derived ingredient. Full ingredient and allergen details will be published with the nutrition panel.

IRON, VITAMIN C, COLLAGEN

What's inside, and why

Is there iron in QT?

Yes. QT is iron-infused and also contains vitamin C and collagen. The ingredient forms and amounts per serving will be on the nutrition panel.

Why pair matcha with vitamin C?

Tea naturally contains tannins, which can bind non-heme iron. QT includes vitamin C alongside its iron. We'll share the full formula and serving details before ordering opens.*

Can QT treat iron deficiency or anemia?

No. QT is a food, not a medicine, and we won't pretend otherwise. If you have a diagnosed deficiency or symptoms like persistent fatigue or dizziness, see your healthcare provider, and keep taking any prescribed supplement as directed. Share the product label with your provider when discussing your routine.

Can I drink QT while pregnant or breastfeeding?

QT contains iron and caffeine. Suitability during pregnancy or breastfeeding is a question for your healthcare professional. The final formula and serving information will be published before ordering opens.
