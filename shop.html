The details.

What's in every packet?

Real matcha with bright fruit flavor, infused with iron, vitamin C, and collagen. Full ingredient and allergen details will be published with the nutrition panel before ordering opens.

How do I make it?

Tear one packet into 8–12 oz of water. Shake or mix well, then sip. Works over ice, in a water bottle, or with your milk of choice.
