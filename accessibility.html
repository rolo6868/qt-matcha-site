Accessibility.

Everyone should be able to read, shop and sign up here. If something gets in your way, we want to know.

Our commitment

QT Matcha aims for qtmatcha.com to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We build with real HTML, keyboard-first navigation, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, text alternatives for images, and dialogs that announce themselves to screen readers. We test with automated tools and keyboard-only use on every release.

What we've done

  • A "skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • All controls reachable and operable by keyboard; menus and dialogs trap focus and close with Escape.
  • Text and background colors chosen to meet contrast requirements, including on colored sections.
  • Images carry descriptive alternative text; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Motion respects your "reduce motion" system setting.
  • Forms have visible labels, clear error messages, and status updates announced to screen readers.
  • Pages work at 200% zoom and on small screens without horizontal scrolling.

Known limitations

Checkout is provided by Shopify and follows Shopify's accessibility practices. Some product photography is decorative and described briefly rather than in full. If you find anything else, the fastest fix starts with an email.

Tell us

If you have difficulty using any part of this site, email sipqtmatcha@gmail.com with "Accessibility" in the subject, or use the contact form. Tell us the page and what happened; we'll respond within 5 business days and work with you to get you what you came for.
